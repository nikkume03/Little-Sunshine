# Little-Sunshine
Little Sunshine is a small, peaceful corner of the internet inspired by...slow living, personal growth, journaling, and finding beauty in everyday moments. I wanted to create a website that feels calm and comforting, like a little reminder to pause, breathe, and appreciate the small things in life.

The website has three pages: a welcoming home page, a journal for little thoughts and reminders, and an About page that shares the story and values behind the project. I chose a minimalist design with sage green, cream, and soft botanical elements to give it a natural, warm, and gentle feel. I also created a logo that represents sunshine, growth, and new beginnings.

I enjoyed experimenting with colours, typography, layouts, and navigation, and discovering how small details can change the entire look and feel of a page.
Through this project, I got to combine creativity with coding and learn by building something of my own. Little Sunshine is more than just a website to me; it represents the idea that growth doesn't have to be rushed, and even the smallest steps can lead to something beautiful.
